# ✈️ PocketPilot

**Know what you can spend today — without losing sight of tomorrow.**

PocketPilot is a student-focused financial planning skill for **Cubbie** that helps students answer two simple questions:

> **Can I afford this right now?**  
> **What will this purchase cost my future goals?**

Instead of requiring complicated budgeting spreadsheets or bank connections, PocketPilot works through simple conversations. Students provide basic financial information, and PocketPilot turns it into clear, actionable guidance.

## 💡 The Problem

Students often have to make financial decisions while balancing rent, bills, financial-aid refunds, paychecks, and long-term goals.

Knowing that you have **$400 in your account** doesn't necessarily mean you have **$400 available to spend**.

PocketPilot helps students understand what their money actually means before they spend it.

## 🚀 What PocketPilot Does

### 💵 Safe-to-Spend
PocketPilot calculates how much a student can safely spend each day based on their current balance, upcoming bills, emergency buffer, and next expected income.

**Example:**

`Safe to spend today: $26`

### 🎯 Goal Tracking
Students can create savings goals such as:

- Graduate school
- Tuition
- A new laptop
- Travel
- Emergency savings

PocketPilot calculates whether their current savings plan will reach the goal on time.

### 🛍️ "Should I Buy This?"
Before making a purchase, students can ask PocketPilot how it would affect both their finances **today** and their future goal.

For example:

> "I'm thinking about buying a $300 concert ticket. How would that affect my grad school goal?"

PocketPilot can show how the purchase affects the student's safe-to-spend amount and how far it could push back their goal date.

### ⚖️ Compare Your Options
PocketPilot can compare multiple choices side-by-side.

For example:

**Buy the $300 ticket now**  
vs.  
**Wait two months**  
vs.  
**Choose a $100 alternative**

This helps students make informed decisions instead of simply being told whether they should or shouldn't spend money.

### 💰 Refund Stretch
When a student receives a large financial-aid refund or other lump-sum payment, PocketPilot calculates how much they can safely spend per day to make the money last until their next expected income.

### 🔔 Smart Heads-Ups
PocketPilot can warn students when their current spending pace could leave them short for an upcoming bill and show how pulling money from savings would affect their goal.

## 🧮 How It Works

PocketPilot uses deterministic calculations rather than guessing financial numbers.

### Daily Safe-to-Spend

`(Balance - Upcoming Bills - Buffer) / Days Until Next Income`

### Goal Projection

`Current Savings + (Monthly Contribution × Months)`

These calculations allow PocketPilot to provide understandable and consistent financial guidance.

## 🔒 Privacy First

PocketPilot is designed without requiring direct access to a student's bank account.

Students stay in control of the financial information they provide, and PocketPilot **does not move, transfer, or invest money**.

It provides educational guidance — the student always makes the final decision.

## 🎓 Built for Students

PocketPilot is especially designed for students managing:

- Part-time income
- Financial-aid refunds
- Tuition and school expenses
- Monthly bills
- Limited emergency savings
- Long-term financial goals

Our goal is simple:

**Help students understand the future impact of today's spending decisions.**
